Open **Home.md**, then **Open the Reader**. The chapter order follows the PDF
and EPUB. Each chapter has ordinary Previous, Home, contents, and Next links;
they work with community plugins disabled. The optional **FirstPair Reader**
plugin adds its own Previous / Up / Back / Top / TOC / Next rail. Enable it only
if you want that interface; the plugin is delivered disabled.

Start with the wiring and power sequence before applying a microphone preset.
Use the B67-269 chapter for its external power supply, and the UT Twin87 chapter
for its 48 V phantom-power path. Starting settings are calibration recipes,
not measurements of your voice. Record gain, distance, polar pattern, filtering,
compressor reduction, and the DAW settings together in **Session log.md** or
**Recall template.md**. Both forms include every column in the comparison CSV
and the extra internal-state, routing and room details needed to reconstruct a
session. **Test script.md** reproduces the book's original comparison passage.
**Worksheets.md** links the editable comparison log and control-sweep CSV files.

Keep personal notes and recordings in a separate personal vault or a copy of
this release. The generated edition is replaced as a whole during an update.
The **Sources** note links directly to the official source-register chapter and
explains provenance; manufacturer manuals remain links,
not redistributed full manuals. The **Visuals** note indexes the included
photographs and original technical illustrations.

The `professional-podcast-audio/_data/units.jsonl` ledger binds the ordered
chapters to manuscript line ranges and content hashes. `_data/reader.json`
drives the optional Reader, and `_data/targets.json` indexes image evidence.
The complete vault works offline; official source links need an Internet
connection. If `Companion/tutorial.html` is included, open that file in a web
browser to use the microphone and DAW selectors.
